# Sections A, D and E: Acceptance, Completion, Reporting and archiving

**Status:** AI draft v0.1, 12 November 2026. Not reviewed. Not approved for use on any engagement.

References are at standard level and from memory; paragraph numbers are for the reviewer to add. These sections are checklists: every step applies to every engagement unless marked "if applicable".

---

# Section A: Acceptance

## A1 Client acceptance and continuance

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1.01 | For a new client, identify the owners, the beneficial owners and management, and verify their identity as anti-money-laundering rules require. Reviewer to add the local requirements. | Identity and ownership records | ISQM 1, ISA 250 |
| A1.02 | Consider the integrity of the owners and management, from inquiries, public information and, for continuing clients, past experience. | Assessment | ISA 220, ISQM 1 |
| A1.03 | For a new client, communicate with the previous auditor, with the client's permission, to ask whether there are reasons not to accept. | Communication and reply | IESBA Code, ISA 510 |
| A1.04 | Confirm the firm has the competence, time and resources to perform the engagement, including any industry or specialist knowledge. | Resource assessment | ISA 220 |
| A1.05 | Consider engagement risks: financial difficulty, disputes with previous auditors, unusual pressure on timing or fees, limitations on scope. | Risk notes | ISA 220 |
| A1.06 | For a continuing client, consider matters arising from the previous audit: disagreements, unpaid fees, scope limitations, changes in ownership or business. | Continuance notes | ISA 220 |
| A1.07 | Partner concludes on acceptance or continuance before any work starts. | Partner sign-off | ISA 220 |

## A2 Independence and ethics

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| A2.01 | Identify threats to independence: financial interests, business or family relationships, long association, fee dependence, gifts, and employment relationships with the client. | Threats checklist | IESBA Code |
| A2.02 | List non-audit services provided to the client, such as bookkeeping, tax, valuation or advisory. Evaluate the self-review and management threats they create and whether each service is permitted. | Services list and evaluation | IESBA Code |
| A2.03 | Record the safeguards applied to each threat, or conclude that the engagement or the service must be declined. | Safeguards | IESBA Code |
| A2.04 | Obtain independence confirmation from each team member. | Confirmations | ISA 220 |
| A2.05 | Partner concludes on compliance with independence requirements. Reconfirm at completion. | Partner sign-off | ISA 220 |

Where the firm also prepares the client's accounts or tax computations, which is common for small entities, A2.02 must be completed with particular care.

## A3 Engagement letter and preconditions

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|

# Section D: Completion

## D1 Summary of misstatements

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1.01 | Accumulate all misstatements identified, other than those clearly trivial. The system collects them from each area, separated into factual, judgemental and projected. | Misstatement schedule | ISA 450 |
| D1.02 | Consider whether the nature or pattern of misstatements suggests that others may exist, and whether the strategy or materiality needs revising. | Assessment | ISA 450 |
| D1.03 | Communicate all accumulated misstatements to management promptly and ask for them to be corrected. | Communication record | ISA 450 |
| D1.04 | Record the misstatements corrected, with the journal entries posted. | Adjusted misstatements | ISA 450 |
| D1.05 | For uncorrected misstatements, obtain management's reasons, and evaluate whether they are material individually or together, considering size, nature, effect on key lines and ratios, and the effect of prior-period uncorrected items. | Evaluation | ISA 450 |
| D1.06 | Communicate uncorrected misstatements to those charged with governance and ask for correction. Include them in the representation letter. | Communication; link to D5, D6 | ISA 450, ISA 260 |
| D1.07 | Conclude whether the statements as a whole are free from material misstatement. If not, see E2. | Conclusion | ISA 450, ISA 700 |

## D3 Subsequent events

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|

| D3.02 | Read minutes of meetings held after the period end and ask about meetings not yet minuted. | Minutes review | ISA 560 |

| D3.06 | If facts become known after the report date that would have changed the report, discuss with management and follow the required steps. | Record, if applicable | ISA 560 |

## D4 Going concern: conclusion

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| D4.01 | Evaluate management's assessment: the period covered, the forecasts and their assumptions, and whether it includes all relevant information known to you. | Evaluation | ISA 570 |
| D4.02 | Where events or conditions cast doubt, evaluate management's plans, test the cash-flow forecast and its key assumptions, and consider facts arising since the assessment. | Forecast testing | ISA 570 |
| D4.03 | Where the entity depends on support from owners or related parties, obtain written confirmation of the support and evidence of the supporter's ability to provide it. | Support letter; ability evidence | ISA 570 |

| D4.05 | Conclude whether a material uncertainty exists and whether the going-concern basis is appropriate. | Conclusion | ISA 570 |
| D4.06 | Confirm disclosures are adequate, and determine the effect on the report. See E1 and E2. | Disclosure review | ISA 570 |

## D5 Written representations

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| D5.01 | Obtain a representation letter from management with appropriate responsibility, addressed to the auditor. The system assembles it from the engagement's findings. | Signed letter | ISA 580 |

| D5.05 | If management refuses, or the representations are unreliable, reconsider management's integrity and the effect on the opinion. | Assessment, if applicable | ISA 580 |

# Section E: Reporting and archiving

## E1 Forming the opinion and the auditor's report

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1.01 | Conclude whether sufficient appropriate evidence has been obtained and whether uncorrected misstatements are material, individually or together. | Conclusion | ISA 700, ISA 330, ISA 450 |
| E1.02 | Evaluate whether the statements are prepared, in all material respects, under the framework: policies disclosed and appropriate, estimates reasonable, information relevant and understandable, disclosures adequate. | Evaluation | ISA 700 |
| E1.03 | Select the opinion. The system summarises open matters and uncorrected misstatements against materiality; the partner selects the opinion. | Opinion decision | ISA 700, ISA 705 |
| E1.04 | Generate the report and confirm it contains every required element: title; addressee; opinion; basis for opinion; going-concern section where required; responsibilities of management and those charged with governance; auditor's responsibilities; other reporting responsibilities required by law; name and signature; address; date. | Report with element checklist | ISA 700 |
| E1.05 | Add any matters that local law requires the auditor to report on. Reviewer to list the UAE requirements. | Legal reporting section | ISA 700, local law |
| E1.06 | Date the report no earlier than the date on which sufficient evidence was obtained, including evidence that the statements have been approved by those with authority. | Approval evidence; date check | ISA 700 |

**Report wording is not drafted.** The illustrative reports in the ISAs are copyright material. The product's report templates should be written once the licensing position is confirmed, then reviewed against the standard and local law.

## E4 File assembly and archiving

| ID | Step | Evidence expected | Reference |
