# Audit Methodology Library

**Status:** AI draft v0.1, 12 November 2026. Not reviewed. Not approved for use on any engagement.

**Scope:** audits of small, non-complex entities reporting under IFRS for SMEs.

## 1. Decisions needed from the reviewer

| # | Decision | Draft position |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Standards basis: full ISAs, or the IAASB's separate standard for audits of less complex entities (ISA for LCE) | Full ISAs. They are accepted everywhere; whether ISA for LCE may be used in the UAE needs checking |
| R2 | Level of reference | This draft cites the standard only (for example "ISA 505"). Paragraph numbers are left for your review, because several standards have been revised recently and paragraph numbers from memory are unreliable |
| R3 | Risk scale | Three levels of inherent risk plus a "significant risk" flag (Section 3) |
| R4 | Controls reliance | Default approach is fully substantive, usual for small entities. Tests of controls are an optional add-on per area |
| R5 | Sampling method | A simple formula driven by risk level and the size of the population relative to performance materiality. Drafted at the end of methodology-B-planning.md; the factors are a proposal |
| R6 | Threshold for scoping an area out of detailed testing | Under 25% of performance materiality, subject to the qualitative conditions in Section 3. The percentage is a proposal, not taken from a standard |

## 2. Library structure

Every engagement moves through five sections. Each item below becomes a checklist or programme in the product.

**A. Acceptance**
- A1 Client acceptance and continuance (ISA 220, ISQM 1)
- A2 Independence and ethics confirmation (IESBA Code)
- A3 Engagement letter and preconditions (ISA 210)

**B. Planning**
- B1 Understanding the entity, its environment and its controls (ISA 315)
- B2 Materiality (ISA 320)
- B3 Preliminary analytical review (ISA 315, ISA 520)
- B4 Fraud risk discussion and assessment (ISA 240)
- B5 Laws and regulations (ISA 250)
- B6 Related parties: identification (ISA 550)
- B7 Going concern: preliminary assessment (ISA 570)
- B8 Risk assessment summary and audit strategy (ISA 300, ISA 315, ISA 330)
- B9 Opening balances, for first-year audits (ISA 510)
- B10 Use of a service organisation, such as outsourced bookkeeping or payroll (ISA 402)
- B11 Need for an auditor's expert or reliance on management's expert (ISA 620, ISA 500)

**C. Fieldwork: one programme per area**
- C1 Property, plant and equipment
- C2 Intangible assets
- C3 Investments and other financial assets
- C4 Inventories
- C5 Trade and other receivables *(drafted below as the sample)*
- C6 Cash and bank
- C7 Related-party balances and transactions
- C8 Trade and other payables, accruals
- C9 Borrowings and lease obligations
- C10 Provisions, contingencies and commitments
- C11 Employee benefits, including end-of-service benefits
- C12 Equity and owner accounts
- C13 Revenue
- C14 Cost of sales and operating expenses, including payroll
- C15 Taxation: corporate tax and VAT
- C16 Journal entries and management override (ISA 240)

**D. Completion**
- D1 Summary of misstatements (ISA 450)
- D2 Final analytical review (ISA 520)
- D3 Subsequent events (ISA 560)
- D4 Going concern: conclusion (ISA 570)
- D5 Written representations (ISA 580)
- D6 Communication with those charged with governance, including control deficiencies (ISA 260, ISA 265)
- D7 Financial statement disclosure checklist (IFRS for SMEs)
- D8 Completion checklist and partner sign-off (ISA 220)
- D9 Comparatives and corresponding figures (ISA 710)

**Specific evidence requirements placed inside areas (ISA 501):** attendance at the inventory count in C4; litigation and claims in C10.

**Excluded from this library:** group audits (ISA 600), internal audit reliance (ISA 610), key audit matters (ISA 701), and special-purpose or single-statement engagements (ISA 800 series).

**E. Reporting and archiving**
- E1 Forming the opinion and the auditor's report (ISA 700)
- E2 Modified opinions, emphasis of matter and other paragraphs (ISA 705, ISA 706)
- E3 Other information, where the entity issues any (ISA 720)
- E4 File assembly and archiving (ISA 230)

## 3. How risk drives the programme

At planning (B8), the auditor rates each area and assertion.

| Rating | Meaning | Procedures loaded |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Simple, routine, little judgement | Core |
| Moderate | Some judgement, volume or history of errors | Core + Extended |
| High | Significant judgement, complexity or incentive to misstate | Core + Extended, with larger samples |
| Significant risk (flag) | Needs special audit consideration | All of the above + Significant-risk procedures; substantive analytical procedures alone are not enough |

**Tiers load per assertion.** Extended and Significant-risk procedures load only for the assertions rated at that level. A procedure that lists several assertions loads if any one of them meets its tier's rating. If only valuation of receivables is rated high, the extended valuation procedures load and the extended existence procedures do not.

**Scoping out an area.** An area may be replaced by a short scoping conclusion only when all of these hold:
- the balance or class of transactions is well below performance materiality (proposed: under 25%, see R6);
- no significant risk or fraud risk has been identified in it;
- it is not qualitatively sensitive (related parties, owner accounts, tax, items affecting covenants);
- the risk is not one of understatement. A small recorded balance never scopes out payables, accruals, provisions, borrowings or related parties, where the concern is what is missing.

Preliminary analytical review (B3) still covers scoped-out areas.

**Assertions used**

| Code | Assertion | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| E | Existence / occurrence | Balances / transactions |
| C | Completeness | Both |
| A | Accuracy | Both |
| V | Valuation and allocation | Balances |
| R | Rights and obligations | Balances |
| CO | Cut-off | Transactions |
| CL | Classification | Both |
| P | Presentation and disclosure | Both |

## 4. Format of every procedure

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | Area code and number, for example C5.04 |
| Tier | Core, Extended or Significant-risk |
| Assertions | Codes from Section 3 |
| Procedure | What to do, in plain imperative wording |
| Evidence expected | What must be on file |
| Reference | Standard relied on |

In the product, each procedure also records work done, exceptions, conclusion, who performed it and when.

## 5. Sample area: C5 Trade and other receivables

**Audit objective:** to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence that receivables exist, are owed to the entity, are complete, are recorded in the correct period, are carried at a recoverable amount, and are properly classified and disclosed.

**Risks commonly found in small entities** (prompts for the planning stage, not conclusions)
- Balances that do not exist or are overstated, where management is under pressure to show revenue or meet loan covenants.
- Inadequate allowance for doubtful debts, especially for old balances and customers in difficulty.
- Sales recorded before the period end that belong to the next period.
- Related-party or owner balances included in trade receivables.
- Credit balances netted off instead of being shown as liabilities.
- Foreign-currency balances not retranslated at the closing rate.

**Notes for the methodology reviewer** (not part of the procedures)
- C5.03: recording why confirmation was not used is proposed as firm policy. Confirm what the ISAs themselves require here.
- C5.13: placed in Core because ISA 540 requires a review of the outcome of prior estimates as part of risk assessment. Confirm.

### Core procedures (all risk levels)

| ID | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C5.01 | C, A | Obtain the customer-by-customer listing with ageing at the period end. Check its arithmetic and agree the total to the trial balance and the lead sheet. Investigate reconciling items. | Listing; reconciliation to TB | ISA 500 |
| C5.02 | E, V, C | Compare the balance, the ageing profile and debtor days with the prior year and with your expectation from revenue and credit terms. Obtain and corroborate explanations for unexpected movements. | Analytical review with expectation, threshold and explanations | ISA 520 |
| C5.03 | E, R | Select balances for external confirmation. Send requests under your own control and have replies come directly to you. Reconcile differences. If confirmation is not used, record why it would be ineffective and what replaces it. | Selection basis; requests and replies; reconciliations; control schedule | ISA 505, ISA 330 |
| C5.04 | E, V | For selected balances not confirmed or not replied to, and as further evidence for others, trace to cash received after the period end, agreeing to bank statements and to the specific invoices settled. | Schedule of post-period receipts tied to invoices and bank | ISA 505, ISA 500 |
| C5.05 | E, A | For selected balances with neither a reply nor later receipt, inspect the invoice, the proof of delivery or service completion, and the customer order or contract. | Copies or references of documents inspected | ISA 500 |
| C5.06 | V | Obtain management's assessment of the allowance for doubtful debts. Understand the method, test the ageing it relies on, and consider old balances, disputes, post-period receipts and known customer difficulties. Conclude whether the allowance is reasonable under the reporting framework. | Management's calculation; your testing of ageing; your assessment per significant balance | ISA 540 |
| C5.13 | V | Compare last year's allowance with what was actually collected or written off since, to judge the reliability of management's estimates. | Retrospective comparison and conclusion | ISA 540 |
| C5.07 | CO | Select sales invoices and credit notes from either side of the period end and agree each to delivery or service evidence, to confirm it is recorded in the correct period. | Cut-off schedule with dates | ISA 500, ISA 330 |
| C5.08 | CL, P | Review the listing for credit balances, related-party balances, staff and owner balances, and amounts due after more than one year. Confirm each is correctly classified. | Annotated listing; reclassification entries proposed | ISA 500, ISA 550 |
| C5.09 | E, V, A | For other receivables, prepayments and deposits, obtain a breakdown, agree significant items to supporting documents and recalculate prepaid amounts. | Breakdown; documents; recalculations | ISA 500 |
| C5.10 | P | Confirm that the disclosures for receivables, the allowance and its movement, and any balances pledged as security agree to the audit evidence and meet the framework's requirements. | Disclosure tie-out; link to D7 | ISA 330, ISA 700 |

### Extended procedures (moderate and high risk)

| ID | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C5.11 | V, A | Test the accuracy of the ageing report by tracing a selection of invoices to their dates and confirming they fall in the right ageing band. | Selection and results | ISA 500, ISA 540 |
| C5.12 | CO, E | Review credit notes issued after the period end and consider whether they reverse sales recorded before it. | Listing of post-period credit notes with assessment | ISA 500 |
| C5.14 | A, V | For foreign-currency balances, recalculate the translation at the closing rate from an independent source. | Recalculation; rate source | ISA 500 |
| C5.15 | R, P | Inspect loan agreements, bank letters and board minutes for receivables pledged, factored or assigned. | Documents inspected; link to C9 | ISA 500 |

### Significant-risk procedures (when flagged)

| ID | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C5.16 | E | Extend confirmations to cover a higher proportion of the balance, and include new customers, large balances raised near the period end, and customers with unusual terms. Verify the confirmation address independently of the client's records. | Extended selection; independent address checks | ISA 505, ISA 240 |
| C5.17 | E, CO | Examine journal entries posted to receivables and revenue near the period end and after it, particularly manual entries and those posted by senior management. | Journal selection with support; link to C16 | ISA 240 |
| C5.18 | E, V | For customers with large or old balances, obtain evidence that the customer is real and able to pay, such as trade licence details, correspondence, and payment history. | Evidence per customer | ISA 240, ISA 500 |
| C5.19 | E, CO | Inspect significant contracts and side correspondence for terms that affect when revenue and the receivable should be recognised, such as rights of return or bill-and-hold arrangements. | Contract review notes | ISA 240, ISA 500 |

### Area conclusion (completed by the auditor)

A standard statement, signed by preparer and reviewer: whether the work was performed as planned, whether sufficient appropriate evidence was obtained, the misstatements identified and carried to D1, and any matters for the partner's attention.

## 6. Review checklist for each drafted area

Use this when you review C5 and every later area.

1. Is every relevant assertion covered by at least one Core procedure?
2. Do the Extended and Significant-risk procedures add evidence, not repeat Core ones?
3. Is each reference the correct standard, and what is the paragraph number? (R2)
4. Does anything contradict the framework, IFRS for SMEs, as it currently stands?
5. Are there UAE-specific points to add, such as corporate tax, VAT, or free-zone requirements?
6. Is the wording clear enough for a junior to follow without asking?
7. Is anything recognisably taken from another firm's or vendor's methodology?
8. Would the evidence listed satisfy a file inspection?
