# Section C: Fieldwork, other areas

**Status:** AI draft v0.1, 12 November 2026. Not reviewed. Not approved for use on any engagement.

Covers C1-C3, C6, C8-C12, C14, C15. References are at standard level and from memory. Tiers: **Core** (all risk levels), **Ext** (assertions rated moderate or high), **Sig** (assertions flagged as significant risk). Assertion codes: E existence/occurrence, C completeness, A accuracy, V valuation, R rights and obligations, CO cut-off, CL classification, P presentation.

Accounting requirements are worded neutrally. The reviewer should check each accounting point against the edition of IFRS for SMEs the client applies, and add UAE-specific requirements where marked.

## C1 Property, plant and equipment

**Audit objective:** evidence that assets exist, are owned, are completely recorded, are carried at appropriate amounts after depreciation and impairment, and are properly disclosed.

**Common risks:** repairs capitalised or assets expensed; assets held in the owner's name; useful lives never revisited; disposed or scrapped assets still on the register.

| ID | Tier | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1.01 | Core | C, A | Obtain the asset register and a movement schedule by class: opening, additions, disposals, depreciation, closing. Check arithmetic and agree to the trial balance and prior-year closing figures. | Register; movement schedule | ISA 500 |
| C1.02 | Core | E, A, R | Select additions and agree to supplier invoices, confirming the amount, the date, that the invoice is addressed to the entity, and that the item is capital in nature. | Additions test | ISA 500 |
| C1.03 | Core | E, R | Physically inspect a selection of assets from the register. For vehicles and property, inspect registration or title documents in the entity's name. | Inspection record; title documents | ISA 500 |
| C1.04 | Core | C | Review repairs and maintenance and similar expense accounts for items that should have been capitalised. | Expense review | ISA 500 |
| C1.05 | Core | E, A | Select disposals and agree to sale documents and proceeds received. Recalculate the gain or loss. Ask whether any assets were scrapped or are no longer in use. | Disposals test | ISA 500 |
| C1.06 | Core | V, A | Assess whether useful lives, residual values and depreciation methods are reasonable and consistently applied. Recalculate depreciation in total by class and compare with the charge. | Assessment; recalculation | ISA 540, ISA 520 |
| C1.07 | Core | V | Consider whether there are indicators of impairment, such as idle assets, damage, losses or a decline in the business. If so, examine management's impairment assessment. | Indicator review | ISA 540 |
| C1.08 | Core | P | Confirm disclosures: policy, reconciliation of movements by class, assets pledged, and capital commitments. | Disclosure tie-out | ISA 330 |
| C1.09 | Ext | C, E | Select assets seen on the premises and trace them to the register. | Floor-to-register test | ISA 500 |
| C1.10 | Ext | A, V | For assets constructed by the entity or built over time, test the costs capitalised and the date depreciation began. Confirm the treatment of borrowing costs follows the framework. | Cost build-up test | ISA 500 |
| C1.11 | Ext | V | Where assets are carried at a revalued amount, or impairment has been tested, evaluate the valuation: the valuer's competence and objectivity, the method, and the key assumptions. | Valuation review; link to B11 | ISA 540, ISA 500 |
| C1.12 | Sig | E, A | Examine large additions near the period end and purchases from related parties: confirm delivery, existence and price. | Evidence per item | ISA 240, ISA 550 |

## C2 Intangible assets

**Audit objective:** evidence that intangible assets meet the framework's recognition criteria, exist, are owned, and are carried at appropriate amounts after amortisation and impairment.

**Common risks:** internally generated costs capitalised when the framework does not allow it; amortisation periods too long; software or licences no longer in use.

| ID | Tier | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C2.01 | Core | C, A | Obtain a movement schedule by class, check arithmetic, and agree to the trial balance and prior year. | Schedule | ISA 500 |
| C2.02 | Core | E, A, R | Agree additions to invoices, contracts or purchase agreements, and confirm the entity holds the rights. | Additions test | ISA 500 |
| C2.03 | Core | E, CL | For each class, conclude whether recognition is permitted by the framework, paying particular attention to internally generated items and development costs. | Recognition assessment | ISA 500 |
| C2.04 | Core | V, A | Assess the useful lives and recalculate amortisation. | Recalculation | ISA 540 |
| C2.05 | Core | V | Consider indicators of impairment, including whether each asset is still in use and generating benefit. | Indicator review | ISA 540 |
| C2.06 | Core | P | Confirm disclosures: policy, useful lives, movement reconciliation. | Disclosure tie-out | ISA 330 |
| C2.07 | Ext | V | For goodwill or assets acquired in a business combination, examine the purchase agreement, the allocation of the price, and the amortisation or impairment treatment. | Acquisition review | ISA 540 |
| C2.08 | Sig | E, V | Where capitalised costs are significant, test the underlying costs in detail, such as timesheets and contractor invoices, and challenge the evidence of future benefit. | Detailed cost test | ISA 240, ISA 540 |

## C6 Cash and bank

**Audit objective:** evidence that cash and bank balances exist, are owned, are complete, and that restrictions and bank facilities are disclosed.

**Common risks:** unrecorded accounts; personal accounts used for business; old reconciling items; cash-in-hand balances that cannot be supported; window dressing around the period end.

| ID | Tier | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C6.01 | Core | E, C, R | Send confirmation requests, under your control, to every bank the entity dealt with in the period, including accounts with nil balances and accounts closed in the year. Request balances, loans, facilities, security, guarantees and signatories. | Confirmation requests and replies | ISA 505 |
| C6.02 | Core | E, A | Obtain the bank reconciliation for every account at the period end. Check arithmetic, agree the bank balance to the confirmation and statement, and the book balance to the ledger. | Reconciliations | ISA 500 |
| C6.03 | Core | E, CO | Trace reconciling items to bank statements after the period end. Investigate items that did not clear promptly and any old items. | Clearance schedule | ISA 500 |
| C6.04 | Core | C | Compare the confirmation replies with the ledger for accounts, loans, guarantees or security not recorded or disclosed. | Comparison | ISA 505 |
| C6.05 | Core | E | Where cash in hand is material, count it at or near the period end in the custodian's presence, and reconcile to the ledger. Otherwise review the balance for reasonableness. | Count sheet | ISA 500 |
| C6.06 | Core | A | Recalculate the translation of foreign-currency balances at the closing rate. | Recalculation | ISA 500 |
| C6.07 | Core | CL, P | Confirm that overdrafts are shown as liabilities unless offset is permitted, restricted balances and deposits with long maturities are properly classified, and disclosures are complete. | Classification review | ISA 330 |
| C6.08 | Ext | CO, E | Review large or unusual receipts and payments in the weeks either side of the period end, including transfers between accounts, to confirm they are recorded in the correct period and not recorded twice. | Review schedule | ISA 500 |

## C8 Trade and other payables, accruals

**Audit objective:** evidence that all liabilities at the period end are recorded, at the correct amounts, in the correct period, and properly classified.

**Common risks:** understatement is the main concern: unrecorded invoices, missing accruals, goods received not invoiced. This area is not scoped out on the size of the recorded balance.

| ID | Tier | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C8.01 | Core | C, A | Obtain the supplier-by-supplier listing with ageing. Check arithmetic and agree to the trial balance. | Listing | ISA 500 |
| C8.02 | Core | C, E | Compare the balance, creditor days and the list of major suppliers with the prior year and with purchases. Investigate suppliers with unexpectedly low or nil balances. | Analytical review | ISA 520 |
| C8.03 | Core | C, A | For major suppliers, selected by volume of purchases and not by balance, reconcile supplier statements to the ledger. Where no statement is available, request confirmation. | Reconciliations or confirmations | ISA 500, ISA 505 |
| C8.04 | Core | C, CO | Search for unrecorded liabilities: review payments made and invoices received after the period end, up to a date close to the audit report, and confirm that those relating to the period were recorded or accrued. | Search schedule with cut-off date | ISA 500 |
| C8.05 | Core | C, CO | Review goods-received records for the last days of the period for deliveries not yet invoiced, and confirm they were accrued. | Cut-off test | ISA 500 |
| C8.06 | Core | C, A | Obtain the accruals schedule. Compare with the prior year and with recurring expenses to identify missing accruals. Test significant items to later invoices or payment and recalculate. | Accruals test | ISA 500, ISA 540 |
| C8.07 | Core | CL, P | Review for debit balances, related-party balances, and amounts due after one year. Confirm classification and disclosures. | Annotated listing | ISA 500 |
| C8.08 | Core | A | Recalculate the translation of foreign-currency balances at the closing rate. | Recalculation | ISA 500 |
| C8.09 | Ext | C | Review open purchase orders and unmatched delivery notes at the period end for liabilities not recorded. | Review notes | ISA 500 |
| C8.10 | Ext | C, A | Review long-outstanding balances and disputed items, and consider whether amounts written back to income are justified. | Review of old balances | ISA 500 |
| C8.11 | Sig | C | Extend the search for unrecorded liabilities: lower the selection threshold, extend the period, and confirm directly with suppliers showing nil or small balances. | Extended search | ISA 240, ISA 505 |

**Common risks:** covenant breaches not reflected in classification; loans from owners with no terms; leases misclassified; security and guarantees not disclosed.

## C10 Provisions, contingencies and commitments

**Audit objective:** evidence that all obligations requiring provision are recognised at a reasonable estimate, and that contingencies and commitments are completely disclosed.

**Common risks:** completeness, as management may not volunteer disputes; estimates biased to manage profit. This area is not scoped out on the size of the recorded balance.

| ID | Tier | Assertions | Procedure | Evidence expected | Reference |

| C10.06 | Core | C, P | Identify capital and other commitments from contracts, orders and minutes. Confirm disclosure. | Commitments schedule | ISA 500 |
| C10.07 | Core | C, P | Compare guarantees and contingent liabilities reported in bank confirmations with those disclosed. | Comparison | ISA 505 |
